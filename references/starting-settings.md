# Print starting settings

Use only after confirming the actual material, nozzle and printer profile. These are starting assumptions, not guaranteed strength or process limits.

- Recorded printer: Anycubic Kobra X, 260 × 260 × 260 mm, Anycubic Slicer Next. Verify the installed nozzle/material. PETG for functional parts and PLA for visual/prototype parts are starting assumptions; service suitability needs evidence.
- FDM wall check: about 1.2 mm for three 0.4 mm perimeters; ribs below 0.8 mm may be unreliable. Resin uses its own process recommendations; hollow parts need drainage and curing access.
- Chamfer the first-layer edge about 0.5 mm for elephant's foot. Fillet internal load-bearing corners.
- Mesh tessellation: 0.01 mm linear and 10° angular deflection are starting values; choose relative to the smallest feature.
- Overhangs about 45–50° are a starting check. Use teardrop holes or bridges where support would spoil a surface; prefer a large flat bed-contact face where loads permit.
- Structural parts: start with 4–5 perimeters and 30–40% gyroid/cubic infill. Cosmetic parts: 2–3 perimeters and 15–20%. Layer height 0.2 mm; 0.12–0.16 mm for fine detail. Verify against the material/profile and intended load.
